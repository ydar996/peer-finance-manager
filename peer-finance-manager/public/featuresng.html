The Money Model

One Ledger. Every Naira Labeled. Every Report Automatic.

Everything flows from one list: the Bank Ledger, a line for every deposit, withdrawal, and charge in the Cooperative's real bank accounts. Each line is labeled with what the money actually was. Every report is just these labeled lines added up, so the books always balance and nothing is ever lost.

💰

Member Money In

Contributions and deposits, share capital purchases, and the one-time registration fee that joins a member (default ₦50,000, set per Cooperative).

🤝

Loans

Disbursements out to members and repayments back in, each tracked against principal and interest separately.

📈

Income and Investment

Interest earned on loans and bank deposits, fixed deposit placements and maturities, and other Cooperative income.

📤

Money Out

Operating expenses, profit distributions to members, and transfers between the Cooperative's own accounts.

Feature by Feature

Every Tool in the Back Office

Organized the way the app is organized: the administrator's tabs. Staff see the same screens read-only; members see only their own portal.

📊

Cooperative Books Dashboard

Live summary cards: deposits this month and year, member contributions, share capital, income, expenses, loans outstanding, expected interest, investments, fixed deposits, bank balances, and net income. Click any card to drill into every transaction behind it, each table ending in a bold total row that always equals the card. Money out is shown in parentheses, the way accountants write it.

🏦

Bank Import and Reconciliation

Export your statement from the bank as CSV or Excel, upload, preview, confirm. Nigerian layouts are recognized automatically: Trans Date or Value Date, Narration, separate Debit and Credit columns, DD/MM/YYYY dates. Duplicates can never double-count, conflicts are flagged, and a Reconcile card keeps a traffic-light check on whether every account still matches the bank.

✏️

Reclassify and Split

A mislabeled line is fixed in seconds: reclassify the whole entry, or split one transfer into parts (part contribution, part loan payment, part fee). Every change is written to an audit trail so the books stay explainable forever.

🧾

Record: Manual Entries

Cash deposits, transfers confirmed outside a statement, adjustments: type them in directly. Manual entries join the same ledger with the same categories as imported ones.

👥

Members and Accounts

A full directory: contact details, photo, member number, contributions balance, share capital, and transaction history. Membership status tracks Active, Resigned, Deceased, Expelled, and Suspended. Payment aliases recognize a member's transfer even when it arrives under a spouse's or business name.

💳

Loans, End to End

Members apply online, guarantors confirm online, administrators approve and set terms. The app builds the repayment schedule, tracks every payment against principal and interest, supports strict or flexible payment policy per loan with optional late fees, and generates a clean PDF loan statement per member per month.

📄

Member Statements

Monthly PDF statements per member, styled like a bank statement: contributions in and out, running balance. Generate one at a time or for the whole membership at once.

📋

Monthly Status Report

The executive-committee document: balance sheet (bank, fixed deposits, investments against member deposits and share capital), income statement (income minus expenses equals net income), per-account bank balances, and custom labels for operating expenses. Publish finished reports so members can read them in their portal.

📅

Meetings

Schedule a meeting once; the app notifies members when it is announced, reminds them before it happens, and tells them if it is cancelled. Notices always reach the member's portal inbox; email delivery is a switch you control.

💬

Messages

Two-way messaging between the Cooperative and its members, inside the app. Announcements and questions without phone numbers, scattered email threads, or WhatsApp groups.

🌐

Public Pages and Applications

Every Cooperative gets a public website with editable About and Bylaws pages and a membership application form. Prospective members apply with no login; administrators review, approve, or decline, and approval can trigger the welcome flow automatically.

📝

Forms and Documents

Integrated with FlexxForms, a companion forms product. Build loan application and membership forms there, link them in the app, and submissions arrive with a full review-approve-track workflow under your own branding only.

🔑

Users and Logins

Create admin, staff, and member accounts. Reset any password with one click; resets are delivered as a one-use, 24-hour secure link, never a password typed into an email. Export a credentials sheet once when handing out member logins.

✉️

Automated Messages and Email

Editable templates for welcomes, deposit confirmations, loan notices, meeting announcements, dues reminders, and report alerts. One master switch, Email Members Automatically, turns all member email on or off; it ships off by default so you opt in deliberately. Annual dues tracking and reminders included.

💼

Subscription and Billing

Your Cooperative's own plan: ₦40,343 monthly, ₦119,818 quarterly, or ₦464,744 annually, with a discount for paying longer. Pay by card online or record a bank transfer. A grace period keeps access running if a payment is late.

🗄️

Maintenance, Backup, Restore

Download the whole database as an encrypted file protected by a passphrase of your choosing, or a plain backup for convenience. Restore accepts either, verifies the file first, and automatically saves the current data before replacing it: a restore can be undone.
