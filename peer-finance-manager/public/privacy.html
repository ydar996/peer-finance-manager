Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Peer Finance Manager ("the Platform") is operated by its platform owner and provides cooperative finance management software to member-owned Cooperatives ("Cooperatives"). Each Cooperative is the data controller for its own members' data; the Platform operator acts as a data processor.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: names, email addresses, usernames, and password hashes for Cooperative administrators, staff, and members.
  • Member data: member profiles, contributions, savings, loans, guarantor details, and transaction records entered by or imported into a Cooperative.
  • Bank data: bank statements and transaction files uploaded by a Cooperative for reconciliation and classification. Narrations may contain payer names.
  • Usage data: session identifiers, IP addresses, and security event logs (logins, changes to sensitive settings) used for auditing and abuse prevention.
  • Payment data: subscription billing is processed by Stripe. Card numbers never touch our servers.

2. How We Use Data

  • To operate the Platform: authentication, ledger recording, reporting, and member statements.
  • To secure the service: rate limiting, audit logging, fraud and abuse detection.
  • To communicate service notices to Cooperative administrators.
  • We do not sell personal data. We do not use member financial data for advertising.

3. Data Storage And Processors

Data is stored on infrastructure provided by our subprocessors, including Render (application hosting), Netlify (static hosting), Stripe (payments), and FlexxForms (membership and loan forms). Each Cooperative's data is stored in an isolated database. A current list of subprocessors is maintained in our security documentation and available on request.

4. Retention And Deletion

Cooperative data is retained while the Cooperative's account is active. A Cooperative may request export or deletion of its data at any time; deletion removes the organization's database and stored files. Encrypted backups rotate on the provider's schedule. Security logs are retained for audit purposes for a limited period.

5. Your Rights

Members may contact their Cooperative administrator to access, correct, or delete their personal data held by the Cooperative. Platform account holders may request access, correction, export, or deletion of their account data by emailing the contact below.

6. Security

We protect data with encryption in transit (TLS), hashed passwords, multi-factor authentication support, per-organization data isolation, and access controls. Details of our security program are available on request.

7. Changes And Contact

We will post updates to this policy on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact: privacy@peerfinancemanager.com.
