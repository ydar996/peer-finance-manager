Run Your Cooperative's Finances Without Spreadsheet Chaos

Peer Finance Manager is a secure web platform for member-owned Cooperatives. Treasurers keep the books, members see their own accounts, and the board reviews without changing a number: contributions, loans, bank activity, statements, and month-end reports in one organized system.

Capabilities

Everything a Growing Cooperative Needs in One Place

Cooperative Books

Live cards for bank cash, contributions, loans, CDs, and expenses. Open any card for member-level detail.

Member Accounts/Profiles

Search by name or member number. History, biodata, and downloadable PDF statements on every profile.

Bank Import/Ledger

Import new bank activity, skip duplicates, and catch mismatches before they become month-end problems.

Loans/Repayments

Create loans with guarantors, eligibility rules, and schedules. Members see their own loan activity.

Statements/Reports

Batch monthly contribution statements and Cooperative performance reports you can publish to members.

Meetings/Membership

Announce meetings, collect applications, and publish About/Bylaws pages that need no login.
