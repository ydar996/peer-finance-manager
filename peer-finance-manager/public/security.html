Security and Architecture

Built Like a Vault, Explained in Plain Language

Your Cooperative's books hold your members' life savings. Peer Finance Manager is engineered around one principle: a cooperative's data should be as hard to reach as money in a vault, and as easy to audit as a paper ledger. Here is exactly how it is built, and why that architecture makes it the right home for Cooperative finances.

  • Every Cooperative gets its own private database, not a shared one
  • Passwords are never stored, only their unbreakable fingerprints
  • Backups are encrypted with a passphrase only you know
  • Every change leaves a fingerprint in the audit trail

Architecture

Your Data Lives in Its Own Room, Not a Shared Hall

Most web services put every customer's records into one giant shared database and rely on software filters to keep them apart: a bug or a bad query can expose the wrong rows. Peer Finance Manager takes the stronger approach: each Cooperative gets its own completely separate database file.

🚪

Physical Separation

Your Cooperative's members, ledger, loans, and statements live in a dedicated database file. There are no shared tables to leak through: one Cooperative's data cannot appear in another's screen, report, or backup, by construction rather than by filtering.

🔐

Organization Code at the Door

Every sign-in names its Cooperative with an Organization Code. The server opens only that Cooperative's database for that request and no other, so a session can never wander into a neighbor's books.

🧑‍⚖️

Roles with Real Boundaries

Administrators change data; Staff see everything but can change nothing; Members see only their own account. These checks happen on the server on every request, not just hidden buttons on a page.

🖥️

Runs in the Cloud or on Your PC

The same software also runs as a desktop install for Cooperatives that prefer to keep every record inside their own building. Your data, your premises, your rules.

Credentials

Passwords That Can't Be Stolen, Because They Aren't There

The app never stores a readable password. It stores a one-way scramble using scrypt, the same class of algorithm banks use, tuned to be deliberately slow for attackers even with stolen equipment. Even someone who copied the database could not turn the scramble back into a password.

🐢

Guessers Hit a Wall

Repeated sign-in attempts are slowed and then blocked automatically, and those limits persist across restarts so a server reboot never resets an attacker's free tries. Unknown accounts get the same response time as real ones, so probing cannot reveal who is registered.

🔗

Resets by Secure Link

Password resets arrive as a one-use link that expires in 24 hours and is stored only as a fingerprint. No password ever travels through email, where it could sit in an inbox forever.

🍪

Sessions Scripts Can't Read

Sign-in state rides in browser-protected cookies that webpage scripts cannot touch, and any page trying to submit changes from a foreign website is refused. Changing a password immediately ends every other signed-in session, so a stolen login dies with it.

📱

Authenticator-App MFA

Administrators and staff can require a time-based code from a standard authenticator app at sign-in: something you know plus something you hold.

Your Data, In Your Hands

Encrypted Backups Only You Can Open

Your Cooperative can download its entire database at any time; the data is never held hostage. Downloads can be protected with AES-256, the encryption standard used by governments and banks, sealed with a passphrase that never leaves your head.

🗝️

Your Passphrase, Not Ours

You choose the passphrase at download time; the app never sees or stores it. A lost backup file is useless to anyone who finds it.

🛟

Restores Can't Destroy

Restoring verifies the file is a genuine, intact database before it touches anything, then saves a copy of the current data first. A bad restore can be undone.

🧹

Nothing Sensitive Lingers

Credential exports overwrite themselves so plaintext passwords do not accumulate on the server. Rate-limit records, sign-in sessions, and reset links all expire and are swept away on a schedule.

📡

Verified Integrations Only

Payment and forms events from Stripe and FlexxForms arrive with cryptographic signatures that are checked on every call; unsigned or unconfigured webhooks are refused outright.

Accountability

A Ledger That Remembers Its Own History

Trust in a Cooperative is built on being able to answer "who did what, and when." The app keeps that answer permanently.

✏️

Every Edit Explained

Reclassifying or splitting a bank line writes an audit record: who changed it, when, what it used to say, and the new values. The books stay explainable years later.

✉️

Every Email Accounted

Each automated send, and each one deliberately skipped by the member-email switch, is recorded. You can prove what went out, to whom, and when, or that nothing did.

🧬

Imports Can't Double-Count

Every imported bank line carries a fingerprint. Re-uploading a statement is safe: duplicates are recognized and skipped instead of counted twice.

📮

Member Email Is Opt-In

All member-facing email sits behind a single master switch that ships off. Your Cooperative chooses deliberately before a single member inbox is touched; admin notices and member-requested resets always work.

Ongoing Vigilance

Security That Doesn't Stand Still

The software's building blocks are scanned on a schedule for newly published vulnerabilities, and updates arrive continuously. Pages are served behind a strict content security policy that refuses injected scripts, and security headers tell modern browsers exactly how tightly to wrap every screen.
