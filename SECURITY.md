# Security Policy

## Reporting A Vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in Peer Finance
Manager, please report it responsibly:

- **Email:** security@peerfinancemanager.com
- **Web:** https://peerfinancemanager.com/.well-known/security.txt

Please do not open a public issue for security vulnerabilities.

## What To Include

- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce or a proof of concept
- The affected URL, endpoint, or component
- Your name or handle (optional; we will credit you if you wish)

## Our Commitment

- We will acknowledge receipt within 3 business days.
- We will keep you informed of progress toward a fix.
- We will credit reporters in release notes if you wish to be named.

## Scope

In scope: the Peer Finance Manager web application at
peerfinancemanager.com and its API, the member and admin portals, and
the public cooperative pages.

Out of scope: third-party services we integrate with (Render, Netlify,
Stripe, FlexxForms, email providers), social engineering of our staff,
and denial-of-service testing beyond what is needed to demonstrate a
finding.

## Safe Harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good
faith, avoid accessing or modifying data that is not theirs, and report
findings promptly through the channels above.
