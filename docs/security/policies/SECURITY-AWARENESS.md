# Security Awareness

**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-11-26 | **Owner:** Platform Owner

## For The Platform Owner (annual self-review)

Each year, re-read and attest:

- INFORMATION-SECURITY-POLICY and ACCESS-CONTROL-POLICY
- SECRETS-INVENTORY.md — confirm every secret still current, rotate any
  older than policy
- RISK-REGISTER.md — review every row, update likelihoods, close treated
  risks
- ACCESS-REVIEWS.md — complete one full quarterly review
- INCIDENT-RESPONSE-PLAN.md — walk one scenario mentally or via tabletop

## For Any Future Staff Or Contractors

Before granting access:

1. Complete a 30-minute security briefing covering: phishing recognition,
   credential hygiene, data classification (never put Restricted data in
   chat/email/personal devices), incident reporting channel
   (security@peerfinancemanager.com), and acceptable use.
2. Sign or email-confirm understanding.
3. Record completion date in ACCESS-REVIEWS.md.

## Phishing And Social Engineering Notes

- We never ask for passwords, session tokens, backup passphrases, or env
  secrets by email/chat. Legitimate requests come through the platform.
- Verify sender domains before clicking billing/provider links.
- Report suspected phishing to security@peerfinancemanager.com.
