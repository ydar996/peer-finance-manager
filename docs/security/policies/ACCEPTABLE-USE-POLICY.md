# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-11-26 | **Owner:** Platform Owner

Applies to everyone with access to Peer Finance Manager systems, including
Cooperative admins entering data on behalf of their members.

## Permitted

- Legitimate Cooperative operations: recording contributions, loans,
  expenses, distributions, reconciliation, reporting, member management.
- Testing in non-production environments or with synthetic data.
- Security research reported through the vulnerability channel.

## Prohibited

- Accessing another Cooperative's data or attempting to bypass org
  isolation.
- Uploading malware, unlawful content, or files you are not authorized to
  process.
- Sharing credentials, session tokens, or API keys.
- Scraping, bulk-exporting, or probing the service beyond normal use.
- Using production data in development/test environments without
  anonymization (the Canary org exists for demos: use it).
- Circumventing rate limits, audit logging, or security controls.

## Consequences

Violations may result in access revocation, account suspension, and legal
referral where applicable. Suspected violations are handled per
INCIDENT-RESPONSE-PLAN.md.
